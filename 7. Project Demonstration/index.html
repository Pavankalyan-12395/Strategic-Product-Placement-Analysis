What This Project Does

A comprehensive data analytics initiative designed to transform how retailers think about shelf space and product positioning.

📊
01

Placement vs. Sales Analysis

Analyses the relationship between product placement and sales, helping retailers understand which shelf positions drive better performance.

📈
02

Interactive Tableau Dashboards

Uses interactive Tableau dashboards to present clear and visually engaging sales insights that are easy to explore and act upon.

🎯
03

Zone Performance Identification

Identifies high-performing, average, and low-performing product zones, allowing smarter placement decisions across the entire store floor.

🏪
04

Data-Driven Store Optimization

Enables data-driven store optimization, reducing guesswork and improving overall layout planning with measurable, evidence-backed strategies.

🚀
05

Boost Sales & Customer Experience

Helps boost sales and customer experience by ensuring products are placed in the most strategic and visible locations — turning every shelf into a revenue opportunity.
